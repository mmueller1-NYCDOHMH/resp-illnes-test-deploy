There are 14 wastewater drainage areas in NYC, known as sewersheds. Wastewater from these areas is treated at 14 wastewater resource recovery facilities (WRRFs) located throughout the city. Twice a week, the Department of Environmental Protection collects wastewater from these WRRFs and shares the samples with the NYC Health Department to analyze and quantify biological markers of specific pathogens. Wastewater viral load data are normalized to account for the average daily flow into each sewershed and the approximate population served. SARS-CoV-2 variants are identified in wastewater through whole genome sequencing. Wastewater can provide some information on how much a virus is circulating in our community but may not include or represent all NYC residents infected or those who feel sick or seek medical care.
