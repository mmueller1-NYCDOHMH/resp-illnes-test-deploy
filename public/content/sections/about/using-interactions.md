#### Enlarging a chart on the home page

**Click or tap** any stat card or chart to open a larger version in a modal. Press **Escape** or click outside the modal to close it.

#### Visits vs. hospitalizations

For Emergency Department data, a toggle switches between:

- **Visits** — percentage of ED visits with a diagnosis of the illness.
- **Hospitalizations** — percentage of hospitalizations from the ED with a diagnosis of the illness.

The toggle updates all cards simultaneously.

#### Hovering charts

Hovering over a chart line reveals a **tooltip** with the exact date and value. On a  map, hovering shows that district's name and value.

#### Viewing data by demographic group

Where available, lab-reported case data includes dedicated chart sections for **age group** and **race and ethnicity**. Each shows every group side by side, with a toggle to switch between scaling each group's chart to its own range or sharing one scale across all of them.

#### Sharing a deep link

The browser URL updates as you scroll and switch data types. Copy it at any point to share a link that lands someone directly on the same section and view you're looking at.
